# Anti-Harassment Policy

## Overview
Acme Corporation is committed to a workplace free of harassment and discrimination. This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and visitors, and covers conduct at the office, at company events, on business travel, and in company communication channels.

## Zero Tolerance
Harassment based on race, color, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, age, disability, or any other protected characteristic is prohibited. Sexual harassment, including unwelcome advances, requests for favors, and inappropriate comments or imagery, is prohibited.

## What Harassment Looks Like
Examples include offensive jokes or slurs, unwanted physical contact, intimidation, displaying offensive material, repeated unwelcome invitations, and hostile or demeaning messages in chat or email. A single severe incident can constitute harassment; it does not need to be repeated.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness harassment, report it to any of: your manager, any people manager you trust, HR at hr@acmecorp.com, or the anonymous ethics hotline at 1-800-555-0199. You do not have to confront the person involved, and you do not need proof to report a concern in good faith.

## Investigation
HR will begin an investigation within 5 business days of receiving a report. Investigations are impartial and as confidential as possible. Both the reporter and the accused will be informed of the outcome. Interim measures, such as schedule or reporting-line changes, may be taken during the investigation.

## Non-Retaliation
Retaliation against anyone who reports harassment or participates in an investigation is strictly prohibited and is itself a terminable offense.

## Consequences
Substantiated harassment results in disciplinary action proportionate to the conduct, up to and including immediate termination.

## Manager Duties
Managers who receive a harassment report or observe potential harassment must inform HR within 24 hours. Managers may not promise confidentiality that prevents escalation and may not attempt to resolve harassment complaints informally on their own.
