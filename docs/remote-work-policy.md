# Remote Work Policy

## Overview
Acme Corporation operates a hybrid work model. This policy sets out when and how employees may work remotely while keeping teams connected and productive.

## Hybrid Schedule
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Tuesday and Wednesday are anchor days when all employees in office-based roles are expected on site. Your manager may adjust anchor days for team-specific needs with department head approval.

## Core Hours
Regardless of location, all employees must be available between 10:00 and 16:00 local time for meetings and collaboration. Outside core hours, working time is flexible as long as you meet your total weekly hours and deliverables.

## Fully Remote Arrangements
Fully remote employment requires written approval from your VP and HR. It is generally reserved for roles that do not depend on in-person collaboration and for employees who live more than 75 km from an Acme office.

## Working Abroad
Employees may work from another country for up to 30 calendar days per year, with advance HR approval, subject to tax and immigration constraints. Longer stays require a formal international assignment.

## Equipment and Security
Remote workers must use their Acme-issued laptop and connect through the company VPN when on public or untrusted Wi-Fi. Confidential conversations should not be held in public spaces. See the Information Security Policy and the Equipment Policy for details.

## Home Workspace
You are responsible for maintaining a safe, ergonomic workspace at home. A one-time home office stipend is available under the Equipment Policy.
