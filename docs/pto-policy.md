# Paid Time Off Policy

## Overview
Acme Corporation provides paid time off (PTO) so employees can rest, travel, and handle personal matters. This policy applies to all full-time employees. Part-time employees accrue PTO on a prorated basis.

## Annual Allowance
Full-time employees receive 20 days of PTO per calendar year. PTO accrues at a rate of 1.67 days per month of active employment, starting on the first day of work. New hires may use PTO as soon as it is accrued.

## Carryover
Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 or they are forfeited. PTO does not accrue during unpaid leave.

## Requesting PTO
All PTO requests must be submitted in BambooHR and approved by your manager before the time is taken. Requests for one week or more must be submitted at least two weeks in advance. Requests for shorter periods should be submitted at least three business days in advance. Managers approve requests based on team coverage, on a first-come, first-served basis.

## Blackout Periods
Departments may designate blackout periods during peak business times (for example, the Finance team during year-end close). Blackout periods are announced at least one month in advance. PTO during a blackout period requires director approval.

## Payout on Departure
Accrued, unused PTO is paid out on termination of employment where required by applicable law. Carried-over days that have expired are not paid out.

## Related Policies
See the Sick Leave Policy for illness-related absences and the Holiday Policy for company holidays. Sick days and company holidays do not count against your PTO balance.
