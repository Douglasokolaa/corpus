# Code of Conduct

## Overview
This Code of Conduct describes the behavior Acme Corporation expects from every employee, officer, and contractor. It applies at work, at company events, and anywhere you represent Acme.

## Core Principles
Act with integrity: be honest in all business dealings and records. Treat everyone with respect and dignity. Comply with all applicable laws and company policies. Protect company assets and confidential information. Speak up when something seems wrong.

## Respect in the Workplace
Acme does not tolerate harassment, discrimination, bullying, or retaliation. See the Anti-Harassment Policy for definitions and reporting channels.

## Honest Records
Never falsify a document, expense report, timesheet, or business record. Record transactions accurately and in the correct period.

## Confidentiality
Do not disclose confidential company or customer information to anyone without authorization, during or after your employment. This includes conversations in public places and posts on social media.

## Conflicts of Interest
Avoid situations where your personal interests conflict with Acme's interests, and disclose potential conflicts promptly. See the Conflict of Interest Policy for gift limits and disclosure rules.

## Reporting Violations
Report suspected violations to your manager, HR, or ethics@acmecorp.com. You may also use the anonymous ethics hotline at 1-800-555-0199, available 24/7. Reports are handled confidentially to the extent possible.

## Non-Retaliation
Acme prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation. Retaliation is itself grounds for discipline.

## Consequences
Violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment, and where relevant, referral to authorities.
