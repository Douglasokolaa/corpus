# Equipment Policy

## Overview
Acme Corporation provides the equipment employees need to do their jobs. Company equipment remains Acme property and must be handled per this policy and the Information Security Policy.

## Standard Issue
Every employee receives a company laptop (MacBook Pro or ThinkPad, based on role), a monitor, keyboard, and mouse for office use. Engineering and design roles may request higher-spec hardware through the IT portal with manager approval.

## Laptop Refresh
Laptops are refreshed every 3 years, or earlier if the device can no longer be secured or repaired economically. IT contacts you when your device is due for refresh; old devices must be returned for secure wiping.

## Home Office Stipend
Employees receive a one-time home office stipend of $500 to purchase a desk, chair, or other home office equipment. Submit purchases through Expensify with receipts, tagged "Home Office Stipend". The stipend does not renew when you change roles.

## Peripherals and Accessories
Additional peripherals (headsets, webcams, adapters) up to $150 per year may be ordered through the IT portal without special approval. Requests above that amount need manager approval.

## Care, Loss, and Theft
Take reasonable care of company equipment. Report loss or theft of any company device to IT and security@acmecorp.com within 24 hours so it can be remotely locked and wiped. Accidental damage should be reported to IT for repair; employees are not charged for accidental damage.

## Personal Use
Incidental personal use of company laptops is permitted. Do not install unlicensed software, cryptocurrency miners, or file-sharing tools, and do not let family members use the device.

## Return of Equipment
All company equipment must be returned within 14 days of your last day of employment, using the prepaid shipping kit IT provides. See the Offboarding Policy.
