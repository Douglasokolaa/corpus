# Sick Leave Policy

## Overview
Acme Corporation provides paid sick leave so employees can recover from illness or injury, attend medical appointments, or care for an immediate family member who is ill. Sick leave is separate from PTO and does not reduce your PTO balance.

## Annual Allowance
Full-time employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year, available in full on January 1 (prorated for new hires). Unused sick days do not carry over to the next year and are not paid out on departure.

## Notification
If you are unable to work due to illness, notify your manager by 9:30 AM local time on each day of absence, by message or phone. If you become ill during the workday, tell your manager before leaving.

## Medical Documentation
A doctor's note is required for absences of more than three consecutive working days. The note must confirm you were unable to work; it does not need to include a diagnosis. Notes are submitted to HR, not to your manager.

## Increments
Sick leave may be taken in half-day increments, for example for a medical or dental appointment.

## Extended Illness
If you exhaust your sick leave during an extended illness, contact HR to discuss short-term disability benefits or unpaid medical leave. Acme complies with all applicable family and medical leave laws.

## Abuse of Sick Leave
Sick leave is for genuine illness. A pattern of unexplained absences adjacent to weekends or holidays may trigger a review by HR.
