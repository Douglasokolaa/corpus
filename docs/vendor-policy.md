# Vendor and Procurement Policy

## Overview
This policy governs how Acme Corporation selects, onboards, and pays vendors and suppliers. It exists to control spend, manage risk, and keep vendor relationships fair and transparent.

## Vendor Onboarding
Before any work begins or data is shared, a new vendor must: sign a mutual NDA, complete the vendor registration form with banking and tax details, and pass a security review by the Security team if they will access company systems or data. Vendors processing personal data must also sign a data processing agreement per the Data Privacy Policy.

## Purchase Orders
A purchase order (PO) is required for any purchase over $5,000. Purchases over $25,000 require three competitive quotes documented in the procurement system, or a written sole-source justification approved by the Finance director. Purchases over $100,000 require CFO approval.

## Approval Thresholds
Managers may approve purchases up to $5,000. Directors up to $25,000. VPs up to $100,000. CFO approval is required above $100,000. Splitting a purchase into smaller invoices to avoid a threshold is prohibited.

## Invoicing and Payment
Vendors submit invoices to ap@acmecorp.com referencing a valid PO number. Standard payment terms are net-45 from receipt of a correct invoice. Early payment discounts should be taken when the discount exceeds 1% for payment within 15 days.

## Vendor Reviews
Critical vendors (those handling confidential data or supporting production systems) are reviewed annually for performance, security posture, and contract compliance. Procurement maintains the critical vendor register.

## Conflicts of Interest
Employees involved in vendor selection must disclose any personal or financial relationship with a bidding vendor and recuse themselves from the decision, per the Conflict of Interest Policy. Gifts from vendors follow the $100 disclosure rule in that policy.

## Termination
Vendor contracts must include termination-for-convenience clauses where possible. On termination, vendors must return or destroy company data and certify destruction within 30 days.
