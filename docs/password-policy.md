# Password Policy

## Overview
Strong, unique credentials are the first line of defense for Acme Corporation systems. This policy applies to all accounts used for company business.

## Password Requirements
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases of several random words are encouraged because they are long and memorable. Passwords must be unique per system: never reuse a password across services, and never reuse your Acme password for personal accounts.

## Password Manager
All employees must use the company-provided password manager (1Password) to generate and store work credentials. IT provisions your 1Password account during onboarding.

## Rotation
Routine forced rotation is not required. You must change a password immediately if you suspect it has been compromised, if it was shared, or if Security instructs you to.

## Sharing and Storage
Never share passwords, including with IT or your manager. IT will never ask for your password. Do not write passwords down, store them in plain text files, or send them in chat or email. Shared team credentials, where unavoidable, must live in a shared 1Password vault.

## Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA is mandatory on all company systems, as set out in the Information Security Policy. Use the approved authenticator app or a hardware security key. SMS-based MFA is allowed only where no other option exists.

## Compromise
If you believe a credential is compromised, change it immediately and report the incident to security@acmecorp.com within 24 hours, per the Information Security Policy.
