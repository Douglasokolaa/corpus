# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Overview
Acme Corporation reimburses reasonable, necessary business expenses. Spend company money as carefully as you would spend your own.

## Submitting Expenses
Submit expenses in Expensify within 30 days of incurring them. Expenses submitted more than 30 days late may be rejected. Each expense needs a business purpose description, and reports are approved by your manager.

## Receipts
Itemized receipts are required for any expense over $25. Credit card statements alone are not sufficient. Missing receipts require a signed missing-receipt affidavit and Finance approval.

## Meals
While traveling on business, meals are reimbursed up to a per diem of $75 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Client entertainment is reimbursed up to $150 per person per event and requires director pre-approval.

## Non-Reimbursable Items
The following are never reimbursed: personal purchases, traffic or parking fines, airline seat upgrades beyond policy, minibar charges, hotel movies, and expenses for spouses or partners.

## Payment
Approved reimbursements are paid with your regular payroll within two pay cycles of approval. Reimbursements are made to your payroll bank account.

## Corporate Cards
Employees who travel more than four times per year may request a corporate card from Finance. Corporate card charges must still be submitted and coded in Expensify within 30 days.

For flights, hotels, and mileage rates, see the Travel Policy. For vendor invoices and purchase orders, see the Vendor and Procurement Policy.
