# Performance Review Policy

## Overview
Acme Corporation runs a structured performance process so employees get regular, fair feedback and clear growth paths.

## Review Cycle
Formal performance reviews happen twice per year, in April and October. Each review covers the preceding six months.

## Process
Each cycle includes a written self-review, a manager review, and peer feedback from 2 to 4 colleagues chosen by the employee and approved by the manager. Managers hold calibration sessions within their department to ensure consistent standards before ratings are finalized.

## Ratings
Performance is rated on a 5-point scale: 1 (Unsatisfactory), 2 (Needs Improvement), 3 (Meets Expectations), 4 (Exceeds Expectations), 5 (Outstanding). Ratings drive merit increases and bonus multipliers set during the April cycle.

## Goals
Employees set quarterly goals using the OKR framework, agreed with their manager. Goals are recorded in the performance system and reviewed in monthly 1:1s.

## Promotions
Promotion nominations are submitted by managers during either review cycle and evaluated against published level expectations by a department promotion committee.

## Underperformance
A rating of 2 or below triggers a documented conversation with the manager and HR. Continued underperformance leads to a performance improvement plan (PIP) lasting 60 days with weekly check-ins, clear success criteria, and a defined outcome at the end.

## Disputes
Employees who believe their rating is unfair may request a review by the department head and HR within 10 business days of receiving the rating.
