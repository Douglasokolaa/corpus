# Onboarding Policy

## Overview
This policy describes how Acme Corporation brings new employees on board, from offer acceptance through the first 90 days.

## Before Day One
All offers are contingent on a background check completed before the start date. IT ships equipment so it arrives before day one for remote employees, or has it ready at the desk for office employees. New hires receive a welcome email with day-one logistics one week before starting.

## Day One
New hires complete IT setup, receive their 1Password account, enroll in MFA, and meet their manager and onboarding buddy. HR holds a welcome session covering key policies and tools.

## Onboarding Buddy
Every new hire is assigned an onboarding buddy: a peer outside their direct reporting line who answers day-to-day questions during the first 60 days.

## Required Training
Within 30 days of starting, new hires must complete: security awareness training, anti-harassment training, data privacy basics, and the Code of Conduct acknowledgment. HR tracks completion.

## Benefits Enrollment
New hires must enroll in health insurance and other benefits within 30 days of their start date. Missing the window means waiting for the annual open enrollment except for qualifying life events.

## Probation Period
The first 90 days are a probation period with structured check-ins at 30, 60, and 90 days between the new hire and their manager. Goals for the period are set in writing during the first week. At 90 days, the manager confirms successful completion or discusses concerns with HR.

## First-Week Checklist
Managers must ensure the new hire has: system access appropriate to the role, a written 90-day goal document, scheduled 1:1s, and introductions to key collaborators.
