# Information Security Policy

## Overview
Every employee is responsible for protecting Acme Corporation's systems, data, and customers. This policy defines the baseline security rules that apply to everyone, including contractors.

## Access and Authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required on all company systems. Access follows the principle of least privilege: you get access to what your role requires, nothing more. Access requests go through the IT service portal and require manager approval. See the Password Policy for credential rules.

## Incident Reporting
Report any suspected security incident within 24 hours to security@acmecorp.com. Incidents include lost or stolen devices, phishing you clicked on, malware warnings, or any suspected unauthorized access. Reporting quickly is never punished; hiding an incident is a policy violation.

## Devices
All company laptops have full-disk encryption and endpoint protection managed by IT. Do not disable security tooling. Personal devices may access email and calendar only through the approved mobile management profile. USB storage devices are prohibited unless encrypted and approved by IT.

## Data Handling
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, or Confidential. Confidential data (customer data, financials, source code, personal data) must never be stored in personal accounts, shared via personal email, or pasted into unapproved external tools, including public AI chatbots. Use approved storage: Google Drive company workspace and approved SaaS tools.

## Phishing
Use the Report Phishing button in email for suspicious messages. If you clicked a link or entered credentials, report it to security@acmecorp.com immediately and change your password.

## Training
All employees must complete security awareness training within 30 days of hire and annually thereafter. Completion is tracked by IT and reported to management.

## Clean Desk and Screens
Lock your screen when away from your desk. Do not leave confidential printouts unattended; use the shredding bins for disposal.
