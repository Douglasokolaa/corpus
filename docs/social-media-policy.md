# Social Media Policy

## Overview
This policy guides how employees use social media in relation to Acme Corporation, on both personal and official accounts.

## Personal Accounts
You are free to use personal social media. When you identify yourself as an Acme employee and discuss topics related to our industry or company, include a disclaimer such as "views are my own". Do not create accounts that could be mistaken for official Acme accounts.

## Speaking for the Company
Only the Communications team and executives authorized by Communications may make statements on behalf of Acme. Press inquiries must be forwarded to press@acmecorp.com without comment. Do not announce company news, launches, or partnerships before the official announcement.

## Confidential Information
Never post confidential information: unreleased products, financials, customer names or data, internal metrics, security practices, or details of internal incidents. Screenshots of internal tools, dashboards, or chats are prohibited. See the Information Security Policy for data classifications.

## Respectful Conduct
The Code of Conduct and Anti-Harassment Policy apply online. Do not harass, disparage, or dox colleagues, customers, or competitors. Heated public arguments while identified as an Acme employee reflect on the company.

## Official Accounts
Official Acme social accounts are operated by Marketing. Credentials live in the Marketing shared 1Password vault. Access requires Marketing director approval.

## Endorsements
If you promote Acme products on social media, disclose your employment. If you receive free products or payment from third parties for content related to our industry, follow FTC disclosure rules and the Conflict of Interest Policy.

## Violations
Content that violates this policy may be required to be removed, and violations are handled under the Code of Conduct.
