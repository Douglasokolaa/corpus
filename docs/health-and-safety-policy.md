# Health and Safety Policy

## Overview
Acme Corporation is committed to providing a safe and healthy work environment in its offices and for remote workers. Safety is everyone's responsibility.

## Incident Reporting
Report any workplace injury, illness, or near-miss to your manager and facilities@acmecorp.com within 24 hours. For serious injuries, call emergency services first. HR records all incidents and, where required, reports them to the relevant authority.

## First Aid
First aid kits are located in every office kitchen and reception area. Each office has trained first aiders listed on the safety noticeboard and the intranet. Automated external defibrillators (AEDs) are located at reception in every office.

## Emergency Procedures
Evacuation maps are posted on every floor. Fire drills are held twice per year. When the alarm sounds, leave immediately by the nearest exit, do not use elevators, and gather at the assembly point for a headcount by the floor warden.

## Ergonomics
Employees may request a free ergonomic assessment of their workstation through the IT portal. Recommended adjustments (monitor arms, footrests, ergonomic chairs) are provided for office desks; remote workers may use the home office stipend under the Equipment Policy.

## Workplace Violence
Threats, intimidation, and violence are strictly prohibited. Report any threatening behavior immediately to HR and, if there is immediate danger, to emergency services. Weapons are prohibited on company premises.

## Substance Use
Employees may not work under the influence of alcohol or drugs that impair performance or safety. Moderate alcohol consumption at sanctioned company events is permitted; employees must not drive impaired afterwards.

## Remote Work Safety
Remote employees are responsible for keeping a safe home workspace: clear walkways, safe electrical setup, and an ergonomic desk arrangement. Work-related injuries at home during working hours must be reported the same way as office injuries.
