# Offboarding Policy

## Overview
This policy covers the process when an employee leaves Acme Corporation, whether by resignation, termination, or end of contract.

## Resignation Notice
Employees are asked to give at least 2 weeks written notice. Directors and above are asked to give 4 weeks. Notice is submitted in writing to your manager and HR.

## Exit Interview
HR schedules an exit interview during the final week. Participation is voluntary but encouraged; feedback is anonymized before being shared with leadership.

## Access Revocation
System access is revoked at the end of the employee's last working day. For involuntary terminations, access is revoked immediately upon notification. Managers must submit the offboarding ticket to IT at least 3 business days before the last day where possible.

## Equipment Return
All company equipment (laptop, monitor, badge, corporate card) must be returned within 14 days of the last day, per the Equipment Policy. IT provides a prepaid shipping kit for remote employees.

## Final Pay and PTO
Final pay is issued per applicable law. Accrued, unused PTO is paid out where required by law, per the PTO Policy. Expired carryover days are not paid.

## Benefits After Departure
Health coverage ends on the last day of the month of departure. Departing US employees receive COBRA continuation information from HR within 14 days.

## Ongoing Obligations
Confidentiality obligations survive employment. Departing employees must return or delete all company data and may not take customer lists, source code, or internal documents. Questions about post-employment restrictions go to legal@acmecorp.com.

## Knowledge Transfer
Managers must ensure a written handover document is completed before the last day, covering active projects, key contacts, and credentials moved to shared vaults.
