# Conflict of Interest Policy

## Overview
A conflict of interest arises when your personal interests could interfere, or appear to interfere, with Acme Corporation's interests. Most conflicts are manageable if disclosed early.

## Disclosure
Disclose any actual or potential conflict to your manager and ethics@acmecorp.com as soon as you become aware of it. HR and Legal will determine whether the conflict must be eliminated or can be managed with safeguards. An annual conflict disclosure form is completed by all employees each January.

## Gifts and Entertainment
You may accept modest business courtesies. Any gift, meal, or entertainment worth more than $100 from a vendor, customer, or partner must be disclosed to ethics@acmecorp.com, and may need to be declined or donated. Cash and cash equivalents (gift cards, vouchers) may never be accepted in any amount. Never accept anything offered to influence a business decision, and nothing may be accepted during an active procurement involving the giver.

## Outside Employment
Outside employment or consulting is permitted if it does not compete with Acme, use company time or resources, or impair your performance. Disclose outside work to your manager and HR before starting it. Working for a competitor, customer, or vendor requires written approval from your VP.

## Financial Interests
Disclose any material financial interest (over 1% ownership or $10,000) in a competitor, customer, or vendor held by you or an immediate family member.

## Relatives and Personal Relationships
Relatives and partners may work at Acme but not in the same reporting line. Employees may not participate in hiring, performance, or pay decisions about a relative or partner. Disclose such situations to HR so reporting lines can be adjusted.

## Board Seats and Advisory Roles
Serving on the board of another company, including nonprofits with business ties to Acme, requires prior CEO approval.

## Procurement Decisions
Employees involved in vendor selection must disclose any relationship with a bidding vendor and recuse themselves from the decision, per the Vendor and Procurement Policy.
