# Parental Leave Policy

## Overview
Acme Corporation supports employees welcoming a new child through birth, adoption, or foster placement. This policy provides paid, job-protected leave in addition to any statutory entitlements.

## Eligibility
All employees who have completed 90 days of employment are eligible. Where statutory schemes provide greater benefits, the statutory scheme applies and Acme tops up pay to 100% of base salary for the durations below.

## Leave Amounts
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid leave. Primary caregiver means the parent who has the majority of caregiving responsibility during the leave period; each family designates this themselves.

## Timing and Flexibility
Leave may begin up to 2 weeks before the expected birth or placement date and must be completed within 12 months of the birth or placement. Leave may be split into up to two blocks with manager and HR agreement.

## Gradual Return
Employees may return part-time at 60% schedule and full pay for the first 4 weeks after parental leave, with manager approval.

## Benefits During Leave
Health insurance, retirement contributions, and equity vesting continue as normal during paid parental leave. PTO does not accrue during any unpaid extension.

## How to Apply
Notify HR and your manager at least 60 days before the expected start of leave where possible. HR will confirm your dates, pay, and benefits in writing within 10 business days.

## Job Protection
Employees returning from parental leave return to the same role or an equivalent role with the same pay and level. Taking parental leave will never negatively affect performance ratings or promotion decisions.
