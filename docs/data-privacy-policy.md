# Data Privacy Policy

## Overview
Acme Corporation processes personal data of customers, employees, and partners. This policy explains how we handle personal data in compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable privacy laws.

## What Counts as Personal Data
Personal data is any information relating to an identified or identifiable person: names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, IP addresses, payment details, and HR records. All personal data is classified as Confidential under the Information Security Policy.

## Principles
We collect only the data we need (data minimization), use it only for the purpose it was collected (purpose limitation), keep it accurate, and delete it when no longer needed. Access to personal data is limited to employees who need it for their role.

## Data Subject Requests
Individuals may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data. Any employee who receives such a request must forward it to privacy@acmecorp.com the same day. The Privacy team must respond to data subject requests within 30 days of receipt.

## Retention
Financial records are retained for 7 years. Employee records are retained for 6 years after employment ends. Customer account data is deleted within 90 days of contract termination unless law requires longer retention. Marketing contact data is deleted on opt-out.

## Data Breaches
Suspected personal data breaches must be reported to privacy@acmecorp.com and security@acmecorp.com within 24 hours of discovery. Where GDPR applies, Acme must notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours, so internal speed matters.

## International Transfers
Personal data may only be transferred outside its origin region under approved safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. Contact the Privacy team before setting up any new data flow.

## Vendors
Vendors that process personal data on our behalf must sign a data processing agreement and pass a security review before receiving any data, per the Vendor and Procurement Policy.
