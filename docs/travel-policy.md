# Travel Policy

## Overview
This policy covers booking and reimbursement rules for business travel on behalf of Acme Corporation. All travel must have a clear business purpose and manager approval before booking.

## Booking
Book all flights, hotels, and rental cars through TravelPerk, the company travel platform. Book at least 14 days in advance whenever possible; bookings inside 14 days require manager justification in the booking notes.

## Flights
Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. For flights of 6 hours or longer, premium economy is permitted. Business class requires CFO approval and is generally limited to intercontinental flights combined with same-day meetings. Choose the lowest reasonable fare within a two-hour window of your preferred time.

## Hotels
The nightly rate cap is $250, or $350 in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich, Singapore, Tokyo). Book standard rooms. Incidentals such as minibar and movies are not reimbursable.

## Ground Transportation
Use public transit, rideshare, or taxis as appropriate. Rental cars should be compact or midsize. When driving your personal car for business, mileage is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile, which covers fuel and wear.

## Per Diem and Meals
Meal reimbursement while traveling follows the Expense Reimbursement Policy: up to $75 per day with itemized receipts for anything over $25.

## Travel Insurance
Acme provides business travel insurance automatically for all trips booked through TravelPerk. For international travel, register your trip so Security can reach you in an emergency.

## Personal Travel Combined with Business
You may extend a business trip for personal time if the added cost is zero or paid by you. Personal days must be marked as PTO.
