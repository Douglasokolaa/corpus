# Holiday Policy

## Overview
Acme Corporation observes paid company holidays each year in addition to PTO. Company holidays do not count against your PTO balance.

## Paid Holidays
Acme observes 11 paid holidays per year:

1. New Year's Day (January 1)
2. Martin Luther King Jr. Day (third Monday in January)
3. Presidents' Day (third Monday in February)
4. Memorial Day (last Monday in May)
5. Juneteenth (June 19)
6. Independence Day (July 4)
7. Labor Day (first Monday in September)
8. Thanksgiving Day (fourth Thursday in November)
9. Day after Thanksgiving
10. Christmas Eve (December 24)
11. Christmas Day (December 25)

## Weekend Holidays
When a holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the preceding Friday. When it falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday. HR publishes the observed holiday calendar each December for the following year.

## International Offices
Employees outside the United States observe their local statutory holidays instead of the list above, up to a minimum of 11 paid holidays per year. Local HR publishes each country's calendar.

## Working on a Holiday
If business needs require you to work on a company holiday, your manager must approve it in advance and you receive a substitute day off to be used within 90 days.

## Holiday Pay
Employees on approved PTO adjacent to a holiday are not charged PTO for the holiday itself. Employees on unpaid leave are not paid for holidays occurring during the leave.
